## 3. Home, Explore and activity participation
- [x] `/home` shows a personalized greeting, avatar, current date and participation prompt; quick actions for publishing, sports, project teammates and dashboard; featured activities; chronological Fresh on campus content with category chips and sorting; community updates; interest/branch/year-based For you recommendations; joined/saved upcoming activities; and a community spotlight. Activity/post tabs distinguish feed types; feed links open detail pages; publish/join/leave changes appear immediately. Empty states are useful and long feeds can Load More or paginate. Sample data is identified as demo content.
- [x] `/explore` displays “Find your next thing.” and “Discover people, projects, games, and experiences happening across JIET.” Search covers title, description, organizer, tags and relevant fields. Category, branch, year, skill, date, location, capacity/open spots and optional everyone/target-group filters apply immediately and combine. Include categories All Activities; Sports and Games; Projects and Technology; Academics and Study Groups; Hackathons and Competitions; Events and Culture; Fitness and Wellness; Creative Arts; Volunteering; Other. Support newest, soonest, most popular and most available sorting; grid/list toggle; result count; visible active filters; clear all; practical state preservation when returning from details; full/open distinction; keyboard operation; mobile drawer/expandable filters; and an empty state linking to Publish.
- [x] `/activities/:id` presents the activity title, description, category, organizer, date, start/end time, location, meeting instructions, required/current participants, available spots, preferred skill level, eligible branches/years, tags, creation date, status and participant list. Desktop has a main content column and participation panel; mobile adapts to one column. The panel shows capacity/spots, organizer, participant avatar preview and constraints; states clearly indicate open/full/cancelled/completed; show Leave Activity for a participant and Manage Activity only for the organizer.
- [x] A student can join an open activity only while capacity remains. Joining is not duplicated; completed/cancelled activities cannot be joined; an organizer cannot accidentally join themselves as a duplicate. Participant count, available spots, participant list, dashboard and related views update immediately, persist, and show success feedback. Leaving similarly updates all related data. Full activities prevent additional participants or expose an explicitly local-only optional waitlist; never exceed maximum capacity.
- [x] Only the activity organizer sees edit/cancel/participant-management controls. Organizer updates are validated. Cancelling preserves participant history and does not accidentally delete history. Non-organizers do not see organizer controls. Activity cards show category, title/short description, organizer avatar/name, formatted date/time/location, participant count/capacity, skill badge when present, remaining-spots indicator, optional cover, and working card/details navigation with responsive consistent sizing.

## 4. Publishing activities and community posts
- [x] `/publish` shows heading “Make something happen.” and subtitle “Bring people together around something you care about.” Both creation modes work: **Create an Activity** and **Share a Community Post**.
- [x] Activity creation requires title, description, category, future date, start time, location and positive-integer maximum participants. Optional fields are end time, minimum participants, skill level (Beginner friendly, Intermediate, Advanced, All skill levels), eligible branches, eligible years, tags, meeting instructions, cover image and whether participants may invite others. Participant constraints cover all JIET students, selected branches, selected academic years and specified skill level.
- [x] Validate mandatory information, future date, positive integer capacity, appropriate organizer participant inclusion and no participant overflow. Show field-level errors and an activity preview before publishing. Submission creates a unique ID, records current student as organizer, initializes participant IDs consistently, defaults to Open, persists in localStorage, shows success toast and confirmation/link, and adds the new activity immediately to Home, Explore and organizer dashboard.
- [x] Community post creation supports required post text plus optional image, related activity and tags; publishes under the current student's profile and appears in the community feed. The author can edit/delete their own demo post; basic likes and comments work if included; changes persist. User-generated content is displayed safely as text, never evaluated or injected as arbitrary HTML.
